title: "Path to AI Technical Architect"
description: "Designing and running AI systems for a whole company: layers, data, security, cost and governance."
---

An AI technical architect decides **how** a company uses AI safely and at scale: which models, where data flows, how it is secured, what it costs, and who is accountable. This track turns the earlier tracks into that big-picture view.

**Before you start:** the AI Agents track, plus Harness and Loop Engineering, make this track much easier.

The track runs in six parts: foundations → building blocks → data → security and governance → operations → delivery.
